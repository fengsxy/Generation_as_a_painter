Uniform policyVisits an even grid. All marks use approximately the same size and a horizontal direction, regardless of material or residual.
Residual policyRanks candidate locations by current RGB error. High-error locations receive the next stroke; direction follows the local target gradient.
Semantic painter policyCombines residual with hand-authored region knowledge: long low-opacity background strokes, tangent hair strands, short skin form marks, olive clothing strokes and fine facial anchors.
WHAT IS AND IS NOT INTELLIGENT
This page uses genuine brush paths and closed-loop error measurement. However, the semantic regions and tool policy are coded by us; a model does not yet invent the observations or intentions. It is a controlled painter-policy baseline for a later autonomous agent.
PSNR is an imperfect pixel metric and may penalize expressive strokes. “Average gain” is the reduction in target RGB mean-square error per accepted stroke. All methods start from the same mean-color ground and receive the same stroke count.
