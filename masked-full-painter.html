EXPERIMENT 11 · WHOLE-CANVAS REPAINT

Paint the entire portrait from incomplete evidence

The painter receives a portrait with one facial region masked. It starts from an empty colored ground and reconstructs the complete image with curved path strokes. Visible pixels guide the marks but are never pasted into the output.
STRICT OUTPUT RULE
No image(ref,...) call is allowed on the painting canvas.
Every visible output element is a background ground or a curved stroke.
Samples inside the mask return null.
The withheld image is used only for evaluation.

CAPABILITY BOUNDARY
This is a complete brushstroke representation generated from a masked observation. Its visible-region fidelity comes from RGB evidence; its hidden-region content comes from a fixed symmetry and continuity prior. A future multimodal agent should replace that prior with explicit observation, hypothesis, candidate rendering and judge-controlled revision.
