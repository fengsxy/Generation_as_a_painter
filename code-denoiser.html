Can brushstrokes act as a denoiser?

The painter may read every noisy RGB value but never the clean target. It starts from an empty ground. Each curved stroke estimates its color from a local noisy neighborhood; its footprint performs spatial aggregation while its direction follows estimated image structure.

WHY THIS IS DENOISING
The input noise is readable; the clean pixels are not. A stroke color is a robust local estimate rather than a target sample. Large footprints reduce variance, while adaptive scale controls bias. The output remains an executable painting rather than a filtered raster copy.
